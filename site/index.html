        Honest caveats
        
    
          
      
        • Three sample briefs prove nothing about accuracy. This shows what the pipeline outputs, not that the numbers are right.
          • 
      
        • A human must review every scope and quote before it goes to a client.
          • 
      
        • The seed history is only 30 past jobs, so estimates are a rough guide and the on-time figure is clamped to 5% to 95%.
          • 
      
        • TabPFN model weights are licensed for non-commercial use only. Selling quotes built with them needs a commercial TabPFN license, or use the baseline estimator.
          • 
      
        • Nothing on this page calls a model. Results are pre-computed and stored in the page.
          •
